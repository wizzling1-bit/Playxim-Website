### Overall Impression
Playxim feels like a premium, state-of-the-art creator platform merging Apple's restrained elegance with Linear's operational speed. The biggest growth opportunity is elevating power-user keyboard ergonomics and guiding first-time creators through their very first upload.

### What's Working
1. **Calibrated Color & Surface Token System**: Dual-mode tokens (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--border-subtle`, `--glow`) create depth without muddy contrast or garish glows.
2. **Dual-Engine Architecture Transparency**: Seamlessly communicates the distinction between arbitrary R2 object storage and adaptive-bitrate video streaming.
3. **Double-Entry Ledger Transparency**: Creator earnings calculator and payout history present clear financial math with zero ambiguity.

### Priority Issues
- **[P2] Global Command Palette & Keyboard Accelerators**: High-volume video creators lack quick keyboard shortcuts (`Cmd+K`, `/` search) to navigate terabytes of media rapidly. (Suggested: `/impeccable delight`)
- **[P2] Guided First-Time Creator Onboarding Empty State**: Empty content states show a basic fallback message rather than a structured 3-step action checklist to spark the first upload. (Suggested: `/impeccable onboard`)
- **[P3] Soft-Delete / 30-Day Recovery Bin**: File deletion is immediate upon confirmation modal; adding a recoverable trash bin provides greater safety for critical creator archives. (Suggested: `/impeccable harden`)
- **[P3] Marketing Navigation Option Grouping**: 7 sibling links in the top navbar push working memory limits; grouping product surfaces under a dropdown maintains focus. (Suggested: `/impeccable distill`)

### Persona Red Flags
- **Alex (Power User / Video Editor)**: Uploads 50+ media assets weekly. Wants a fast `Cmd+K` palette to jump between playlists, folders, and storage buckets without repetitive mouse clicks.
- **Jordan (First-Time Creator)**: Arrives at an empty dashboard after signup. Unsure whether to upload raw camera archives or encoded MP4s first without an onboarding guide.
- **Morgan (Mobile-First Creator)**: Checks earnings and view counts on mobile. Enjoys compliant 44px hit areas, but multi-column analytics tables require card stacking on narrow screens.

### Minor Observations
- Storage usage gauges could feature predictive runway estimates ("Storage full in ~60 days").
- Public share links could offer customizable OpenGraph social preview cards.

### Questions to Consider
- What if the first upload was integrated directly into the signup completion step?
- Could a Linear-style `Cmd+K` palette transform Playxim into the fastest media manager on the web?
